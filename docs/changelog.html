1.0.0 12 October 2026
Current

First release

  • One click on an IMDb title page opens it in VidFast, in a new tab next to yours. Works with movies, TV movies and shorts.
  • Show pages open S1 E1. Episode pages open that exact episode, using the show’s ID rather than the episode’s.
  • If the season or episode can’t be read, the show opens at the start and the icon shows a ?.
  • Remembers your last five titles, one entry per show, and resumes a show from your last episode.
  • Recent titles in a popup on other sites, and in the icon’s right-click menu on IMDb.
  • The icon greys out on IMDb pages with nothing to play, such as people, lists, games and podcasts.
  • Works on desktop, mobile (m.imdb.com) and regional IMDb pages.
