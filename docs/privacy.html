Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

VidFast launcher doesn’t collect, send or sell any of your data. The little it remembers stays in your browser.

What the extension stores

Each time you open a title, the extension saves a short entry so it can show your recent titles and resume shows:

SavedWhy
The IMDb ID of the movie or showTo open it again
Whether it’s a movie or a showTo build the right link
Its titleTo show it in your recent titles
Season and episode (shows only)To resume where you left off
When you opened itTo order the list and show “3 h ago”

It keeps at most five entries. They’re saved with Chrome’s local extension storage on your device only. They aren’t synced to your Google account or other devices.

What it reads

On IMDb title pages, the extension reads the page’s address and the title details IMDb publishes in the page: the type, name, series, season and episode. It does this when you click the icon, and when a page finishes loading so it can grey out the icon on pages with nothing to play. It doesn’t read any other website.

What it sends

Nothing. The extension has no server, no account and no analytics, and it makes no network requests of its own.

When you launch a title, it opens a VidFast page in a new tab, just like clicking a link. What happens on that page is up to VidFast and its own privacy practices.

Permissions, and why they’re needed

PermissionUsed for

Deleting your data

Click × next to a title in the popup to remove it. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log basic visit information such as your IP address, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The font is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
